Getting Started

Pick a module from Home: Cooking, Travel Currency, Shopping Sizes, Home & DIY, or Everyday Units. Type a value on the keypad and see the result in every unit. Metron works offline; only currency rates need a connection, and they are cached and timestamped.

Common Questions

Where is my data stored? On your device only. Metron has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

How do I clear my history? Settings → Privacy: clear all history, or turn history off entirely. Favorites are kept until you remove them.

Where do currency rates come from? A free public exchange-rate API. Rates are shown with their date and are for reference only.
