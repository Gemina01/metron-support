Metron Unit Converter Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

Summary

Metron does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.

Data on your device

Settings, favorites and conversion history are stored locally on your device only. You can clear history or turn it off in Settings → Privacy. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Network use

The only network request Metron makes is fetching public exchange rates for the Travel Currency module. The request contains currency codes only and no personal information or identifiers. Rates are cached on your device with a timestamp.

Tracking and third parties

Metron does not track you and contains no third-party code that collects data.

Children

Metron collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here. Questions: support@geminatech.com
